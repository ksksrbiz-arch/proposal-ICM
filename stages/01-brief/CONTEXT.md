# 01 Brief — root owns selection

Read `docs/batch.md` for a batch. Read the live tracker before choosing leads. Preserve paused, declined, already-contacted and unresolved-identity states. The user may authorize concept work despite launch unknowns; record that distinction explicitly.

Fill `project.json` with schemaVersion, projectId, business name/locality, instruction commit, assignment, scope, builder ID and root reviewer ID. Record each granted action with the user instruction and date. Defaults are false. Permission to build is not permission to publish, send email, alter pricing or change business domains.

Set `outputDir` relative to the private project (normally `site/dist`). Keep source, research, original assets and QA separate. Select the simplest maintainable stack that fits the business, not the last site's stack. Public deployment must be a preview subdomain, not a claimed business domain.

Scope must say `first-site`, `redesign` or `refinement`; first-site eligibility requires checked-source evidence. A Google Site, Square site or business-owned provider storefront is a company website. Our independent proposal is not the company's live site. Do not expand a first-site-only batch to redesign leads without authorization.

Exit: `gate <project> brief`. Handoff: identity to verify, authorized actions, exclusions, owned paths, known conflicts and next research step. No prospect messaging in this stage.
