# 02 Research — evidence before invention

Match name plus phone/location using primary sources where available. Record each source ID, URL, observed date, relevant facts and limitations in `research.json`. Record every planned factual claim with source IDs and `KNOWN`, `INFERRED` or `VERIFY`; only known or explicitly qualified claims may appear publicly. Conflicting facts stay conflicts until resolved. Do not use directory-generated prose as owner testimony.

Audit the current company's navigation, sitemap, footer, major internal paths, booking/menu/shop providers, phone and visit flows. Inventory actual accessible pages with URL, purpose, type and coverage destination. Classify `core`, `detail`, `utility` or `provider`. Count unique meaningful core pages; record inaccessible/unknown areas and checked sources. Status is `known`, `no-company-site-found` or `unknown`, never infer absence from a timeout/403/404. Keep screenshot/HTML evidence private.

The brief must explain: what the business sells, who visits, what they need to decide, what distinguishes this business, its current site's strengths/gaps, its primary conversion, useful existing systems, local constraints and launch inputs. Capture owner voice from real public writing; do not invent a founder story. Date reviews/ratings and quote attribution precisely. Link to the original source; never manufacture review aggregates or a live Google feed.

Build `assets.json`: asset ID, source URL, original private path, SHA-256, kind, subject, identity match, proposed usage, permission/usage basis and `allowedForProposal`. Preserve originals. Downloaded photos must be inspected before selection; a logo refresh must retain requested recognizable features. Label original AI illustrations and proposed brand art; never present them as real customer work or actual interiors. Record photographer/owner approval as a launch dependency when unresolved. A source URL is not a license.

If authentic media is scarce, choose an honest typography/editorial design, permitted exterior image, sourced product detail or labeled illustration. Do not fill the site with stock people, fake results or repetitive decorative cards. Use image generation for useful bespoke graphics only, with its available skill; preserve approved logos/mascots and disclose proposed art.

Exit: research sources, full baseline inventory, claims, differentiators and asset provenance pass `gate research`. No unresolved identity conflict may pass. Launch facts can remain unknown if omitted or qualified in the authorized concept.
