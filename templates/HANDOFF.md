## Business and concept

Identity evidence, differentiators, current company-site strengths and unknowns. Meaningful core-page inventory versus planned/built routes; detail/utility/provider routes separately. Explain the authored visual direction and useful customer journey.

## Proven implementation

Owned source/output paths, reproducible build command, exact public allowlist, preserved logo/mascot and actual integration destinations. What the primary actions actually do. Local-only preparation must not be described as submission or booking.

## Verification

Actual QA/flow/review reports, hashes and screenshots inspected. Every route on mobile/desktop, short-screen cases, keyboard/interaction coverage, unresolved defects. State responsive simulation versus physical-device coverage honestly.

## Publication and tracker

Actual provider deployment ID, timestamp, build stamp, immutable/stable URLs, seal and public verification. Pending is pending. Actual stable-ID tracker readback and preservation checks, or the precise unapplied write plan.

## Owner inputs and next action

Current factual conflicts, content/media approval, permissions, domain/integration ownership, agreed page/price scope and official-launch inputs. Exact next step and owner. Outreach state must come from actual user/mail evidence, not publication.

## Learning handoff

Private learning.json run ID, actual measured outcome, causal finding, before/after experiment, independent review, adopted/retained/held result, architecture promotion commit when applicable and next-run checklist. Failed/blocked delivery remains failed/blocked even when its learning gate passes.
